# Answer-feedback tones

`success.wav` and `failure.wav` are original, nonverbal CroLingo interface
tones generated locally from sine waves. They contain no third-party recording
and are covered by the project license.

The bright success ping rises from 784 Hz to 1175 Hz. The clearly different
failure cue descends from 330 Hz to 220 Hz. Both are mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM
WAV assets with short fades and safe headroom. They are bundled for offline
playback on Android and Linux.
