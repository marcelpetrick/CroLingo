# Answer-feedback tones

`success.wav` and `failure.wav` are original, nonverbal CroLingo interface
tones generated locally from sine waves. They contain no third-party recording
and are covered by the project license.

The success tone combines 880 Hz and 1175 Hz notes. The retry tone combines
294 Hz and 220 Hz notes. Both are mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV assets with
short fades and restrained volume. They are bundled for offline playback on
Android and Linux.
